How to update. Click zone n in a strip to set that chapter to n×10%; click the last filled zone to step back. Arrow keys work when a strip has focus. The 1 and 2 pips cycle not started → in review → processed; ✓ toggles client validation.
Slide counts live on the shaded audience row and apply to all three languages of that audience and segment — enter them once.
 Weighting. Every percentage on this page is a weighted average, not a plain one, so that "50% done" reflects effort rather than a count of ticked boxes. Two factors combine:
  1 · Base language. The NL decks are built from scratch; FR and EN are derived from them. We estimate the base version takes roughly 2× as long, so an NL chapter carries twice the weight of the same chapter in FR or EN. That factor is an estimate — if the translation rounds turn out heavier or lighter than expected, change baseWeight in the myguide-data block and every figure recalculates.
  2 · Slide volume. With Slide-weighted on (top right), each chapter also counts in proportion to its slide count, so a 22-slide chapter moves the needle more than a 9-slide one. Switch it off to weight all five chapters equally. With no slide counts entered at all, this factor has no effect and averages fall back to equal chapters — but while you are part-way through entering them the percentages will look skewed, because chapters still on 0 slides carry no weight. Fill in all five, or work with Equal chapters on until you have.
